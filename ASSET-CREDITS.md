The source license and model identity were verified against the Sketchfab API,
the mirror manifest, and the embedded USD metadata. The calibration cube was
removed. The plant was normalized, converted to glTF, and simplified for distant
instances. Its photographic texture is baked scan appearance, not measured PBR.
All three levels of detail are real triangle geometry, not photographic planes.

Moon and water textures: Three.js examples, MIT.
